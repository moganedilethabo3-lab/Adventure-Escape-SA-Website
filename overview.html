Why you should Choose Us!

Adventure Escape SA offers a practical mix of outdoor activities, structured packages, and tailored experiences for individuals, families, and groups. Our system is designed to help customers compare options and make bookings without feeling overwhelmed.

The company provides a balance of excitement and preparation, allowing guests to select activities that match their interests, fitness level, and trip goal. The adventure packages include short, high-energy activities that suit both casual explorers and team-based challenges.

Typical adventure settings


Adventure Escape SA's main focused adventure settings are the following outdoor scenery locations:

  • Mountains
  • Ocean /Sea
  • Forest
  • Beach
  • Hotel / Resort
  • Out in the Karoo for wildlife spotting

Escape locations are planned around suitable outdoor locations that permit team-building and recreational adventure that allow both individuals and groups of individuals

Escape Packages are not only for enjoyment but an opportunity to also experience the fun of being Outside in Nature.
