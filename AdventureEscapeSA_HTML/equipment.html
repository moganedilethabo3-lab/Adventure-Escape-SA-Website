Equipment guidance

We recommend that guests prepare appropriately for each activity. The information below is intended as general guidance to help visitors understand what to bring and what may be useful for different outdoor adventures.

Suggested equipment by activity

  • Ziplining Adventure: secure footwear, comfortable outdoor clothing, sun protection, and a light daypack.
  • Kayaking Experience: quick-dry clothing, water-friendly footwear, sunscreen, sunglasses, and a reusable water bottle.
  • Rock Climbing Session: climbing-friendly shoes or sturdy trainers, flexible activewear, gloves if preferred, and a light hydration pack.
  • Outdoor hiking or mountain routes: layered clothing, hat, trail backpack, and weather-ready outerwear.

General preparation checklist

  • Water bottle and hydration plan.
  • Weather-appropriate clothing and layers.
  • Sun protection such as sunscreen and a hat.
  • Comfortable walking or climbing shoes.
  • Small bag for essentials and personal items.

Tip: Ask the company about any specific safety or technical requirements before arriving.
