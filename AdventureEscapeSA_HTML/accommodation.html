Accommodation suggestions

This page offers general accommodation ideas for guests planning a trip around an outdoor adventure. Adventure Escape SA does not provide evidence that it directly owns or partners with accommodation providers, so this content is presented as practical suggestions for trip planning rather than an official company accommodation offering.

Guests may wish to stay near adventure venues, forest areas, or mountain destinations to make travel easier before and after activities.

Accommodation types to consider

  • Guesthouses and lodges: Comfortable for family or small group stays near outdoor venues.
  • Self-catering cottages: Good for group organisers who need flexibility and shared spaces.
  • Camping or glamping sites: Suitable for guests wanting a more immersive outdoor experience.
  • Hotel stays in nearby towns: Practical for travellers who want easier access to activities and transport.

What to look for

  • Proximity to the activity venue.
  • Secure parking or easy access for equipment.
  • Space for family or team groups.
  • Comfortable storage for outdoor gear.
  • Flexible check-in times for early bookings.
