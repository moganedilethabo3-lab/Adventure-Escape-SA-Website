Overview

Adventure options designed around you

Adventure Escape SA offers a practical mix of outdoor activities, structured packages, and tailored experiences for individuals, families, and groups. Our solution is designed to help customers compare options, understand the standard adventure offerings, and enquire about custom experiences without feeling overwhelmed.

The company provides a balance of excitement and preparation, allowing guests to select activities that match their interests, fitness level, and trip goal. The available adventure format includes short, high-energy activities and full-day packages that suit both casual explorers and team-based challenges.

Adventure options

  • Individual activities: Ziplining Adventure, Kayaking Experience, Rock Climbing Session.
  • Packages: Ultimate Adventure Day, Family Explorer Package, Mountain Adventure Package, Corporate Team Challenge.
  • Suitable for: couples, families, solo travellers, friends, and corporate teams.
  • Experience style: scenic, active, confidence-building, and memorable.

Pricing overview

  • Individual activities: R750 each
  • Adventure packages: R1,500 each
  • Discounts: Based on number of bookings, confirmed by the company.
  • Standard discount tiers apply to multiple bookings: 5% for 2, 10% for 3, and 15% for 4+ bookings.

Typical adventure settings

Adventure Escape SA focuses on natural settings such as forest trails, mountain terrain, river routes, and scenic outdoor spaces suitable for team-building and recreational adventure. These environments allow for both excitement and practical outdoor learning.

Activities can be planned around location selection, weather conditions, group preferences, and availability. We encourage guests to request a personalised booking for a more tailored experience.
